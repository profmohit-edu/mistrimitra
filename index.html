Ask in your language. Get a sourced answer. Raise the work order by voice.

A real-time voice agent on Gemini 3.8 Live for maintenance technicians. It searches English manuals, SOPs, past work orders and shift chats, answers in Hindi, Hinglish or your language with the source named, and acts in the maintenance system only after you say yes. Demo plant: Shakti Auto Components, Pune (fictional).
